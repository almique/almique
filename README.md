## 💼 What I Do at Intuit

- 💸 **QuickBooks Payment Links**: building the backend that lets small businesses create and share payment links and get paid faster.
- 🧩 **Templatization**: reusable, configurable templates so payment and invoicing experiences can be built once and reused across many flows.
- 🤖 **AI-native fintech**: putting LLMs and agentic workflows into financial products, from intelligent automation to AI-assisted experiences for customers.
- ⚡ **Event-driven backend**: resilient, Kafka-backed services built for QuickBooks Online scale.
